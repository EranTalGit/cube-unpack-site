Privacy Policy

Cube Unpack · Last updated October 26, 2026

Cube Unpack ("the game") is made by Eran Tal. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number.

Information we collect

  • Game progress such as your level, coins, gems, harbor and settings. It is stored on your device.
  • Nickname and avatar you choose. If you take part in the Cube Cup, your nickname and score are shared with other players on the leaderboard under an anonymous player ID.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them; it receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt, never your payment details.
  • Usage analytics. We use PostHog to see how the game is played (for example which levels are hard) so we can improve it. Events are tied to an anonymous ID, not to you.
  • Advertising. We show ads through Google AdMob. If you allow tracking when the game asks (App Tracking Transparency), AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you do not allow it, ads are not personalized. In regions that require it, the game asks for your consent before showing ads.

How we use it

To run the game, save your progress, deliver purchases, show the leaderboard, show ads and improve the game. We do not sell your information.

Third parties

  • Apple (App Store payments): apple.com/legal/privacy
  • RevenueCat (purchases): revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (ads): policies.google.com/privacy
  • PostHog (analytics): posthog.com/privacy
  • Supabase (Cube Cup leaderboard): supabase.com/privacy

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn off ad tracking in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. You can turn off reminders in the game settings or in iOS Settings, Notifications. Deleting the game removes the progress stored on your device. To ask us to delete leaderboard data, write to us at the address below.
