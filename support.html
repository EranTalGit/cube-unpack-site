Cube Unpack Support

We are happy to help

Questions, a bug, or a purchase that did not arrive? Email us at eran14777@gmail.com and include your device model and the level you are on.

Common questions

I bought something and did not get it. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. If it is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I remove ads? Buy No Ads in the Shop. It removes the ads between levels forever and can be restored on a new device.

I am stuck on a level. Use a Hint to see the best next cube, a Magnet to pull matching cubes, or an Extra slot for more room.

How do I turn reminders off? Settings in the game, then Reminders. You can also turn them off in iOS Settings, Notifications.
